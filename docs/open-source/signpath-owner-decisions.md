## Contact route

**Primary: support@signpath.io, email, routing-only Option A first.**
The [official support page](https://signpath.io/support) explicitly presents email
for first interactions or individual requests; its support portal separately
requires registration. This is a published general SignPath support route, not
confirmed delivery to Foundation eligibility reviewers. The
[Foundation About page](https://signpath.org/about) says the Foundation is currently
operated by SignPath GmbH; this supports asking the company for routing but does
not establish who reviews eligibility.

**Fallback: info@signpath.io, email**, published for SignPath GmbH on the
[official contact page](https://signpath.io/contact). Its form invites questions,
but email avoids unnecessary form/account steps. Use only after separate approval
of the exact recipient/channel/message; no automatic escalation or duplicate send.

**SIGNPATH_CONTACT_ROUTE_UNVERIFIED** remains the Foundation-specific status.
The [route-by-route evidence](signpath-preliminary-review-2026-10-30.md#official-contact-routes)
covers all inspected official pages. Neither mailbox explicitly promises
preliminary Foundation eligibility handling. Email does not require a SignPath
login as published; any provider-side ticket creation/routing is unknown.

The [Foundation application](https://signpath.org/apply) is a formal subscription
application with account-contact fields and required Code of Conduct/data consent.
Its fields were visible in this retrieval; the earlier retrieval limitation stays
recorded in dated evidence. The page is publicly readable without login, but this
does not authorise submission or establish the complete later account process.
The terms' support address for violation reports does not establish eligibility
routing. No email, form, account, ticket, upload or terms consent was performed.
