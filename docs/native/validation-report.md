Security scan 5ccf4c42-57c8-4923-ac76-02084b4c0bbc is complete and sealed for all 309 tracked files in src/native at b45246e. Its report records two source-validated low-severity availability findings: DOCX relationship aliases retain duplicate image arrays, and repeated image references expand export payloads beyond unique-resource budgets. Both require explicit local import/export. Open XML Framework 3.5.1 inspection confirms alias reachability; no exhaustion exploit was executed. Declared-DEFLATE-size and CSV-formula hypotheses were rejected with recorded counterevidence. The scan was sequential and source-only, with no independent sub-agent audit or online advisory search. Web, legacy-shell, installer and CI sources were outside its requested scope. The sealing warning notes the docs-only HEAD change; scoped product source was unchanged during the scan.

Subsequent engineering corrections deduplicate imported parts by URI and bound each image to 32 MiB, retained images to 64 MiB and unique images to 1,024. Export preflight charges every image, diagram and prepared formula occurrence, including repeated references, up to 32 Mi characters of base64 and 4,096 occurrences; rendered copy uses 8 Mi characters. HTML generation has a bounded writer; DOCX/PDF package output has a 128 MiB bounded stream. Word reuses image parts and PDF reuses immutable image-source strings. Cancellation is checked during import copying, export traversal and output writes; MigraDoc's synchronous rendering call cannot be interrupted internally. Selected-table validation excludes unrelated document images. Small-budget regressions verify rejection without excessive allocations, ordinary repeated-image fidelity, schema-valid Word, actual PDF image/text inspection and unchanged source.
