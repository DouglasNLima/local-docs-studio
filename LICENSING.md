The root [LICENSE](LICENSE) is the operative, unmodified MIT licence for the
verified original first-party portions of the R1/R2 files enumerated in the
[grant manifest](docs/open-source/configuration/mit-scope.json). The approved
notice is **Copyright (c) 2026 DouglasNLima**. The owner confirmed individual
ownership of his original material and authorised this notice and restricted
scope in [Goal #62](https://github.com/DouglasNLima/local-docs-studio/issues/62).
This is an owner declaration, not independent legal verification of title.

The licence operates for this feature-branch copy. Adoption on `develop` requires
the separately authorised merge; the Goal #62 receipt records that branch state.
It does not relicense earlier tags or third-party material. A hosting service's
automatic MIT badge must not be read as a licence for the entire repository.

## Exact grant and exclusions

| Boundary | Treatment |
|---|---|
| R1 — implementation | MIT for enumerated original browser application, native WPF application, original legacy shell source, tests, build tooling and configuration; embedded exclusions below always apply |
| R2 — documentation | MIT for enumerated original first-party documentation and policy prose; quotations, upstream notices and uncleared embedded examples retain their own rights |
| R3 — examples | Samples, fixtures, registries, templates, snippets and embedded sample material are excluded by default, even inside R1/R2 files; no individual R3 clearance is added here |
| R4 — visuals and branding | Logos, names, brand identity, icons, PNG/SVG/ICO/XAML artwork, screenshots and other visuals are excluded; copyright clearance and trademark permission are separate; MIT grants no branding endorsement |
| R5 — evidence | Preserved audits, recorded screenshots, historical captures, upstream copyright evidence and generated receipts retain their provenance and original rights |
| R6 — upstream and adapted content | Dependencies and adapted files retain their actual grants, copyright and notices; they are outside this first-party grant |
| R7 — user content | Private/customer/user-imported or created workspaces, documents, templates, assets and exported user content retain their owners' rights; use of the studio transfers no rights |

R6 includes `assets/vendor/**` except the verified project-authored
`assets/vendor/manifest.json` catalogue; pinned/adapted Mermaider (including
`src/native/LensDocsStudio.Core/Rendering/BoundedErLayout.cs`), Sugiyama/dagre,
NuGet dependencies, .NET/WPF runtimes, WpfMath/Computer Modern/OFL fonts, Inno
components and legacy Windows App SDK/WebView2 binaries. Preserve dual-licence
choices and all original notices. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
Excluded does not mean unlicensed: an upstream grant can independently apply.

Mixed files are licensed only for their original first-party portions. Path
membership never converts embedded third-party code, examples, quotations or
artwork to MIT. In particular, test assertions/harnesses are distinct from their
embedded sample payloads, and WPF control logic/layout is distinct from vector
artwork. `CommandIcons.xaml` and dedicated template/fixture resources are excluded
as whole files. Generated installers and assemblies can combine multiple rights;
neither the complete installer nor all compiled resources are labelled MIT.

## Inspecting and maintaining the boundary

The [path ledger](docs/open-source/evidence/goal62/rights-ledger.csv) binds each
current path to canonical Git bytes (SHA-256), classification, grant status and
the applicable content exclusions. It is regenerated by
`python scripts/compliance/rights_ledger.py`; its own digest is supplied by the
final Git tree to avoid a self-reference. The allowlist is explicit: a new R1/R2
path is BLOCKED until reviewed and added to the grant manifest. Generation cannot
clear a new path, verify ownership or override an exclusion.

The reviewed input is Goal #60's frozen ledger at
`0890ec9b2ade8af24b9b3fca6cbe98cd7fd329ba`, narrowed where content review identified
additional examples/templates, plus the enumerated original Goal #62 work.
Git history, bounded attribution scans and unchanged dependency/notice bytes
support that review; they do not prove originality of every embedded fragment.
Unclear material remains BLOCKED for first-party grant or binary release, as
applicable. See [the Stage A receipt](docs/open-source/goal62-mit-receipt.md).

Redistributions of covered portions must retain LICENSE and this scope disclosure
along with the applicable upstream notices. Package metadata points here with
`SEE LICENSE IN LICENSING.md`; it does not assert a single licence for the binary
distribution. MIT permits third-party commercial reuse and resale. Free official
distribution, without subscriptions or paid feature tiers, is project policy,
not an additional restriction on MIT recipients.
